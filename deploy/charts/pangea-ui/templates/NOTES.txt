pangea-ui deployed.

1. Wait for it to come up:

     oc rollout status deployment/pangea-ui

2. Open the dashboard:

     oc get route pangea-ui -o jsonpath='https://{.spec.host}{"\n"}'

The UI reverse-proxies /api to the pangea-api Service, so the backend does not
need to be reachable from outside the cluster for the dashboard to work.
